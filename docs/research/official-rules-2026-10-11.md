# FinTechathon 2026: official International Track rules (as read 2026-10-11)

## Attachment 2, "International Track - Topics & Rules" (authoritative; dated 2026-10-08)

Downloaded from https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/templates/international-topics-rules-en.docx (the
registration dialog on the English site links it). Where it differs from the web page below, this
document is the more detailed one.

- **Topic F | Agent Wallet - Autonomous On-chain Wallet:** "The track's original on-chain topic -
  build an AI wallet agent that autonomously executes on-chain operations (asset management,
  transaction execution, risk control). Ambitious ideas are encouraged, with a 0-5 point challenge
  bonus."
- Scoring: task completion 40%, security & compliance 30%, innovation & interaction 30%,
  "Challenge bonus (on-chain exploration): 0-5 points". "Choosing the on-chain topic does not affect
  scoring."
- **Deadline for registration AND submission: 20 October 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8) =
  16:00 UTC.** A bilingual reminder email is sent 24 hours before. Submissions may be updated any time
  before the deadline; the latest version received prevails; no changes after the deadline.
- Submission checklist:
  1. Technical documentation (system architecture, core algorithm description, security design)
  2. Presentation deck (final presentation no longer than 10 minutes)
  3. Demo video (optional, no longer than 5 minutes; the web page says required, so we deliver one)
  4. Source code (with README, GitHub repository link, deployment instructions, and test cases)
  5. Security self-assessment report (permission-tier implementation and known-risk list)
  6. Execution evidence (on-chain topic: real on-chain transaction hashes)
- Review: late October by WeBank industry experts and university scholars; finalists by 31 Oct;
  20 Nov check-in and WeBank HQ visit; 21-23 Nov Grand Final in Shenzhen. At least one member of
  each finalist team must attend in person.
- Prizes: First RMB 100,000; Second 80,000; Third 50,000; Best Innovation Award 20,000; several
  Excellence Awards; outstanding teams get fast-track interviews at WeBank.
- Contact in the document: info@ican-x.com (registration goes to fintechathon@ican-x.com).

## Registration (Attachment 3, registration form)

No web form for the International Track: download
https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/templates/international-registration-form-en.xlsx, complete it in
English (one form per team) and email it to **fintechathon@ican-x.com** with the subject
"International Track Registration - Team Name - Team Leader Name". A confirmation reply comes within
48 hours.

- Team: team name*, topic (A-F)*, entry title (provisional), advisor (optional), proposal summary
  (optional, 100-200 words), team email*.
- Each member: role (leader/member)*, full name*, gender*, age*, nationality*, passport/ID type and
  number*, student ID*, university*, major*, year of study*, image of the passport bio-page*, proof
  of enrolment attached (student card / enrolment certificate)*, email*, phone/WhatsApp.
- Declaration signed by the team leader. Participants must be 18 or older.
- The filled form holds personal data: it lives in `private/` (git-ignored) and is never committed.

Source: https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/#/en/rule (International Track tab) and the home page
https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/#/en. The site states that the Chinese version prevails in case of
discrepancy.

## The competition

- 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition, WeBank FinTech College Students Challenge (ninth
  edition; "held for eight consecutive editions").
- Strategic guidance: Shenzhen Municipal Local Financial Administration, Shenzhen University, WeBank.
  Host: Shenzhen University-WeBank Institute of FinTech. Organisers: iCAN Future (Beijing) Technology
  Co., Ltd., GouXiongHui. Technical advisers: CCF Technical Committee on Digital Finance, Tsinghua
  Institute of Financial Technology, IEEE CIS Singapore Chapter, IEEE CIS Shenzhen Chapter.
- Theme of the International AI Track: "AI as a Financial Participant". "Select one direction and
  deliver a working financial Agent that demonstrates intent understanding, task planning, safe
  execution, user control and measurable value in a financial scenario."
- Directions A-E are new or extended this year; "Direction F is the original Agent Wallet":
  "F. Agent Wallet: An AI-owned wallet that can spend on-chain".

## International Track rules

- Eligibility: full-time undergraduate, master's and doctoral students at universities or research
  institutes outside China (including Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan), any nationality, major or year.
- Teams of 2-5 members; at least 60% non-Chinese nationals (rounded up).
- Registration: every member's name, nationality, passport or other ID type and number,
  institution, proof of enrolment, major, year of study and contact details. One team per person.
- English is the official language; materials must include an English version.
- Third-party data, code, open-source components and APIs must be legally licensed.
- Intellectual property stays with the team; the organiser may use entries for non-commercial
  publicity (non-exclusive, royalty-free).
- Governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
- Contact: fintechathon@ican-x.com

## Judging (same dimensions for all six topics)

| Weight | Dimension | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| 40% | Task Completion | "Completion of scripted tasks in a simulated environment for non-on-chain topics; Topic F is evaluated using on-chain transaction hashes." |
| 30% | Security & Compliance | "Defence against induced transfers, data extraction, privilege escalation and prompt injection; serious security failures trigger substantial deductions." |
| 30% | Innovation & Interaction | "Scenario novelty, real-world value, dialogue efficiency, clarification, correction and fallback capability." |

"A capped 0-5 point challenge/implementation bonus rewards advanced topics or demonstrated
real-world deployment. Topic choice itself does not affect the base score. Appeals may be filed
within three business days after results are announced."
