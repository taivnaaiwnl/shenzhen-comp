**Fact-check: Agent Wallet (Topic F) in earlier editions, and what past winners built. Checked 2026-10-11.**

**Answer in brief**
- No earlier FinTechathon edition (2023, 2024, 2025) had an Agent Wallet or on-chain wallet topic, so there are no past Agent Wallet winners to learn from.
- The "original" Agent Wallet is the 2026 International Track's own first announcement (June 2026). It started as one theme and five directions were added later.
- The closest earlier topics are:
  - **2023 and 2024 blockchain tracks.** All three 2024 blockchain winners built on FISCO BCOS. Their projects were about traceability, verifiable computation and copyright. None was about wallets, payments or agents.
  - **2025 Data Analysis track.** Its first reference topic was an SME credit-scoring model.

**CONFIRMED**

1. **The 2025 (7th) edition had only two tracks, AI and Data Analysis.** No source mentions blockchain, on-chain, a wallet or Agent Wallet.
   - Facts: launched 26 Sep 2025; 1,709 students, 372 teams, 270 universities; RMB 500,000 in prizes; final 5-7 Dec 2025.
   - Nuance the researchers missed: there was no international track, but students from overseas universities could enter ("国内外高校在读生...均可报名参赛", students at universities in China or abroad may enter).
   - Sources: https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-10-09/doc-infthnhk9684233.shtml ; https://www.peopleapp.com/column/30051004536-500007261769 ; https://m.tech.china.com/redian/2025/1209/122025_1778234.html
   - Confidence: high.

2. **The 2025 AI track brief named three areas:** "智能风控、金融产品智能推荐、AI驱动的客户服务" (smart risk control, smart product recommendation, AI-driven customer service).
   - The Data Analysis track gave four reference topics. Topic 1 was "基于多维度数据的小微企业信用评分模型" (an SME credit-scoring model built from multi-dimensional data). That is the nearest past topic to our Agent Credit Bureau. The researchers did not mention it.
